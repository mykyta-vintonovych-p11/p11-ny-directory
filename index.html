HOW TO READ THE LIST

Scale first.
Relevance second.

Firms are ordered by indicative global scale within each sector, then filtered for a confirmed staffed office in the New York metro. The order is a working outreach aid—not a financial league table.

Verified means the address is supported by an official company page. Emails are never guessed. If a useful public email is unavailable, the card links to the official contact route.

Always arrange a meeting before visiting. Re-check contact details if more than 90 days have passed since the verification date.
